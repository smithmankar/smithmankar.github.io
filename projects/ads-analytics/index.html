01

The brief

The agency runs Meta ads for Verte and Alexandre Christie (watches), Season Earth (linen menswear), THS (real estate) and HarboCharge (EV charging), with no in-house performance marketer.

The job: run the accounts, and know what is really working. That means Shopify orders, leads the sales team can call and site visits they can book, not the purchases an ad platform claims for itself.

02

What I did

  • Account structure, launches, budget proposals and kill or keep reviews for every brand.
  • A reporting standard, and 36 reports built on it for Verte, Alexandre Christie, Season Earth, THS and prospects.
  • Lead and abandoned-cart pipelines that put every lead in front of the sales team within seconds.
  • Audits that follow results past the click: leads to site visits, failing funnels, and our own change logs.
  • Rebuilt the customer lists behind four brands' lookalike audiences.
  • Market research: a repeatable way to size competitors from the ad libraries, and a festive-season brief on the Indian ad market.
  • Tools: SparrowOS analytics, MCP servers that let Claude query SparrowOS and Reddit Ads, and AdPilot, an assistant where the engine proposes, a person confirms and code executes.

03

How I did it

A standard before a verdict+

Ads are judged on the funnel the platform actually measures (reach, frequency, CTR, CPM, clicks, spend and days live), benchmarked per market, since US CPMs ran about 13 times India's. Attributed ROAS can confirm a call but never leads one. Ads are judged at the ad-set level, because an ad with no sales is often the first touch that makes the next one sell.

Structure that keeps learning+

One ad set per product per market, with location as a hard rule. No verdict until days live and spend give enough evidence. Market tests are built as paused drafts for approval, so nothing goes live by accident.

Reports that check themselves+

Orders come from Shopify, never from an ad platform. A script re-derives every figure in a report and checks it against the document before it ships, and every report goes out as HTML plus a single-page PDF.

Leads in seconds, not days+

Meta lead ads arrive through a webhook whose signature is checked, get answered fast, then are written to the brand's sheet from a queue with retries. A daily sweep reconciles anything missed. 627 leads have gone through it since 22 July.

Follow the money past the click+

For THS, 90 days of leads were traced to site visits campaign by campaign. For Season Earth, a one-click checkout widget skipped the cart entirely, so add-to-cart was the wrong thing to measure; most people saw an ad only once, and broken tracking had starved the retargeting pool. For Verte's US Reddit test, three weeks of spend had produced no sales by Shopify's count or Reddit's own, and the rebuild got pass marks set in advance.

Fix the audiences under everything+

Every brand's lookalikes turned out to be built from one shared list that was almost entirely one brand's customers. I rebuilt each brand's seed list with a spend value for value-based lookalikes, plus a deduplicated pool of 16,919 people across brands.

Decision: check our own edits first

When results drop, the first suspect is our own change log: budget moves, rebuilds and audience edits, each lined up against the day-by-day numbers. Only then do we look at the platform or the market.
