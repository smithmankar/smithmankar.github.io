Verte India storefront

For Verte

Verte's Indian store, built and run end to end: a heavily customised Dawn theme built around a freeform layout system and a 3D product stage for the Project Karbon launch.

verte.co.in
RESULTS
  • Verte's monthly orders went from about 30 (Feb to May) to over 300 (Aug and Sep 2026) while we ran its stores and ads (Shopify)
  • About 150 Diwali sign-ups in the page's first three days, at roughly ₹15 of ad spend each
WHAT I BUILT
  • Urban Canvas: place text, images and video at exact coordinates with parallax, in desktop, mobile and hero variants
  • Moved checkout from Shopflo to Shiprocket with our own buy buttons after the vendor's theme hid them
  • Diwali registration page posting to Google Sheets through Apps Script, with a consent line and a Meta registration event
  • Monthly EMI shown on product pages, worked out in Liquid from the payment gateway's rates
  • Warranty claim page wired to SparrowOS
