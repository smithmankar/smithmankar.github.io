SparrowOS

For Sage Sparrows

The operations platform Sage Sparrows runs on. It replaced scattered Google Sheets and WhatsApp threads with one place for tasks, chat, KPIs and analytics across every brand the agency manages.

RESULTS
  • In daily use at os.sagesparrows.in: on 2 Sep 2026 it held 9 brands, 56 campaigns, about 110 assets and 120 open tasks
  • Replaced Google Sheets and WhatsApp as the way the team runs work, with productivity and progress tracking up by about 70%
WHAT I BUILT
  • Tasks with a reviewer-driven stage flow, real-time chat (threads, mentions, reactions, file sharing), Google Calendar sync and global search
  • Built-in mail and drive on the company's own Stalwart server, private notes with share links, and a daily standup board
  • KPI calculator with weighted department scoring, MD scores and profit sharing
  • Daily pipelines from Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Shopify, Microsoft Clarity and Google Trends feeding per-brand revenue, traffic, conversion and ad dashboards
  • Ad evaluation engine that sorts every ad into test, kill, optimise, breakeven or scale
  • Six-tier roles, TOTP-gated impersonation, web push with retries and batching, and a WebSocket build id that reloads clients after a deploy
  • Plain-English and voice commands across 47 tools, with an undo log and single-use plan tokens
