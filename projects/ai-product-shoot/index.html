AI product shoot pipeline

For Sage Sparrows clients

A Python suite that takes a product from raw photos to a live Shopify listing: AI model shots, AI descriptions, upscaling, resizing and upload.

What I built

  • Gemini (Nano Banana Pro) generates six lifestyle variations per product from one model photo and one product photo
  • Gemini writes SEO titles and HTML descriptions from the raw images
  • 4x upscaling locally with SwinIR and GFPGAN face restoration, or through the Topaz API
  • Resizes to 3:4, compresses to a target size and uploads through the Shopify Admin API, all from one interactive menu

Deep dive

AI pipeline that takes product data + model reference images and generates 6 professional variations per product using Gemini's image generation.
