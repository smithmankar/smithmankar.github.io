Applicant rating, a fundraising bot and outreach automation for a nonprofit's internships.

Rates internship applicant quality by embedding their free-text responses and comparing them against a labelled training set using cosine similarity.

  • Training set of 1,240 labelled responses, embedded with a local DeepSeek model through Ollama
  • Accuracy: about 89% match
  • Profanity detection with flexible regex that catches spaced and symbol-broken spellings in English and Hindi: 47 responses flagged
  • Results go back into the team's sheet for review
