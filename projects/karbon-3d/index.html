Project Karbon 3D viewer

For Verte

A real-time 3D Karbon for Verte's product pages, with a working movement behind the exhibition caseback and hands that tell the viewer's own time.

WHAT I BUILT
  • The second hand steps 8 times a second, matching the Miyota 9015's 28,800 vph, with every hand checked against seven clock times
  • Balance wheel oscillates at exactly 4 Hz; the rotor has inertia and is spun by dragging, as on a wrist
  • Gears turn at speeds inverse to their radius, neighbours counter-rotating, and the hairspring coils instead of spinning
  • Blender prep: a fused 113,000-vertex fittings mesh split into 93 parts, gears named by size and the missing balance wheel built
  • Materials made physically valid (the crystal had been an opaque metal), and the washed-out look traced to an HDR used as the backdrop
  • Section-scoped three.js viewer, about 1,860 lines, with a fisheye sheet of review cards around the watch
