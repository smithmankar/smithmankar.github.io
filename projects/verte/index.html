01

The brief

Verte makes watches: URBN, the everyday line, and Project Karbon, carbon-fibre pieces on a Miyota 9015 (the Series) or a Sellita SW200-1 (the Speciale). It sells in India on verte.co.in and to the rest of the world on vertewatches.com. From February to May 2026 the brand averaged about 30 orders a month.

We rebuilt the site almost from scratch. The old theme's basic code and designs were thrown out, and every page a shopper sees was built again around the brand's launches. From there the job was the whole digital side: both stores, every page and launch, the 3D, the checkout, the tracking, the ads, and the numbers to prove what worked, measured on Shopify orders rather than on what an ad platform says it sold.

03

How I did it

Start again, then build for launchesJanuary to April 2026+

We threw out the old theme's basic code and designs and rebuilt the store. The launches needed exact, art-directed layouts, so I built Urban Canvas first and used it for the home page and the Karbon, Speciale and URBN pages. On the Speciale page the hero's watch travels into the product gallery as you scroll and lands on the matching photo, following whichever colour is selected.

3D, from the model to the pageApril to July 2026+

The 3D stage paints every scene into one shared canvas and blends them as you scroll, so the team can arrange 3D moments in the theme editor. A second engine spins two models around one centre with real HDR reflections, inside a fisheye wall of customer reviews that scrolls forever and opens any review on a tap.

A global store with its own checkout19 May 2026+

Shopify Payments isn't available to Indian merchants, and without it Shopify can't charge in any currency but rupees. So the global store got its own checkout: a Stripe session in USD, then a webhook that writes the order back into Shopify as paid, so inventory and fulfilment carry on as before. It went live on 19 May, with a one-line DNS rollback.

Run the ads like a systemJune to August 2026+

One ad set per product per market, location as a hard rule, and no verdict on an ad before days live and spend give enough evidence. Orders rose from 175 in June to 275 in July and 309 in August, with Shopify sales running at 10 to 12 times Meta spend.

A Karbon that tells the timeJuly 2026+

The Karbon's 3D model came with its movement fused into one 113,000-vertex mesh. In Blender I split it into 93 parts, named the gears by size and built the missing balance wheel. In the browser the second hand steps 8 times a second like the Miyota 9015, the balance wheel swings at 4 Hz, the rotor spins when you drag it, and the hands show the viewer's own time.

Catch the numbers lying26 and 27 September 2026+

Meta showed 12 global purchases over two days when Shopify had 3 orders. The thank-you page was firing a purchase for cancelled and declined redirect payments, and again on every reload. I fixed it the same day: the page now checks the payment's real status, fires once per order, and tells a customer whose payment failed exactly that.

Explain the "alternate day" ordersLate September to October 2026+

Orders started dropping on some days. I checked Meta, GA4, Shopify, the checkout's own logs and Clarity: the ads and traffic were normal. On 5 of 14 days, the checkout provider's payment step stopped completing for hours. Shoppers who chose a payment method paid 30% of the time on those days against 63% on others, which cost 18 to 25 orders. The test-checkout monitor came out of this, along with a second checkout provider that uses our own buy buttons.

Checkout and payments, made switchableOctober 2026+

The India checkout moved from Shopflo to Shiprocket with our own buy buttons, then became a theme setting, so the provider can change without code. Pay-later and card EMI amounts are worked out in Liquid from each gateway's own rates and match the checkout on all 94 plans checked. Instagram's and Facebook's in-app browsers completed payment 31% of the time against Chrome's 61%, so those shoppers now get a card to reopen the store in their browser with the cart carried over, run as a 50/50 test.

Search and the festive seasonOctober 2026+

Google search brings 16% of the India store's sessions but 41% of its purchases, so an SEO pass went live on both sites on 7 October: brand-first titles, meta descriptions, Product structured data with shipping and returns, hreflang between the two stores and one H1 per page. For Diwali, a registration page I built from the team's design, with a sparkler that follows the cursor, collected about 150 sign-ups in its first three days at roughly ₹15 (16 US cents) of ad spend each.

A new watch, from brief to CADOctober 2026+

Verte's next watch is ours to design: a 38 mm titanium piece around a Ronda quartz movement. Dial directions, case styles, a 20-finish dial library and straps were decided one step at a time over eight rounds. The case is factory CAD written in Python, a STEP assembly of six named parts, and a one-file offline configurator shows each round in 3D.

Decision: Shopify is the source of truth

Every result here is a Shopify order. Ad platforms are used for attribution only, and when Meta and Shopify disagree, the job is to find out why rather than report the bigger number.

04

What it changed

Verte now runs on a store rebuilt around its launches, sells worldwide in its own currency through a checkout it owns, shows its watches in live 3D, and judges its ads on real orders. When something breaks, a monitor says so within 15 minutes instead of a slow week saying it later.
