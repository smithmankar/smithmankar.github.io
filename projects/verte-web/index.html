Verte global storefront

For Verte

vertewatches.com: a headless Next.js port of Verte's Shopify theme that sells worldwide in USD through its own Stripe checkout.

vertewatches.com
RESULTS
  • 96 orders through its own Stripe checkout from launch on 19 May to 23 Oct 2026 (Shopify count, team orders included)
WHAT I BUILT
  • The theme's JSON templates are imported and rendered in React, so pages keep the same sections and settings as the Shopify store
  • Stripe checkout with orders created back in Shopify, and USD prices read from variant metafields
  • Geo split that sends Indian visitors to verte.co.in while keeping both sites indexable
  • SEO pass: sitemap, robots, Product and Organization JSON-LD with ratings and shipping policy, hreflang and H1s
  • Fixed the layout shift that was failing Core Web Vitals (CLS 0.52 on phones) by measuring every element with and without media
  • Review stars and a region-aware payment and pay-later line on every watch page
  • Host allowlist, Cloudflare WAF and authenticated origin pulls against a live reverse-proxy clone of the site
  • Fixed a thank-you page that reported unpaid checkouts to Meta as purchases
  • About 43,000 lines of TypeScript
